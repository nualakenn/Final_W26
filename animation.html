The Story of Elm

The Story of Elm

2D Animation | Procreate, Photoshop, Clip Studio Paint | 4:10

Elm: Navigating a Dying World is a 2D-animated short that explores loneliness and grief through the lens of nature. Like humans, trees are social beings. They grow and experience hardship in ways similar to ours. Both humans and trees get sick, both need sunlight and water, and both grow and adapt to their surroundings. These similarities help people to connect with nature through appreciation and sympathy. As global warming and other disasters like diseases often make it feel like the world is ending, it is important to take a moment to appreciate the good in nature around us on a daily basis.

Through Elm, I hope for people to find a connection to the world around them, and the next time they step outside, they might take more time to feel the sun on their faces and hear the breeze brush through the leaves. The story aims to inspire people to understand that despite hardship, life goes on. For every tree that is cut, a new one will sprout up.

Solace

Solace

2D Animation | Photoshop | 1:28

Solace holds particular significance for me as one of the first animated shorts I created. Developed during a period of profound isolation following the pandemic, the piece reflects my desire for connection through a narrative that others could relate to.

As one of my first animated projects, it presented a range of creative and technical challenges. Produced entirely in Adobe Photoshop at 12 frames per second, the process pushed me to strengthen my patience, refine my storytelling, and deepen my commitment to the craft.

The Doorway

Doorway

2D Animation | Photoshop | 0:45

This rotoscoped peice was an experimental project for a class I took in 2022. The Doorway is a reflection of my thoughts while navigating college and my path forward.

By taping clips of myself in my doormroon, then editing and animating on top of the video, I created this piece.
